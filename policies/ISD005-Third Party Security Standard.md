    particular' are deemed to have the words \"without limitation\"
    following them.

    subordinate legislation shall (except where the context otherwise
    requires) be construed as referring to such legislation as amended
    and in force from time- to-time and to any legislation which
    re-enacts or consolidates (with or without modification) any such

    Telecommunications Security Code of Practice.

6.  The Supplier will, in accordance with Good Industry Practice:

<!-- -->

a)  have in place (and regularly and thoroughly test at its own cost and
    expense), security arrangements to:

<!-- -->

i.  protect the access, availability, integrity and confidentiality of
    Community Fibre Data and any other information relating to Community
    Fibre and any of its officers, agents, professional advisers,
    employees, or subcontractors which is disclosed to, or processed or
    otherwise handled by, the Supplier, any of its personnel or
    subcontractors; and

ii. ensure that such information is not:

> **A.** other than in accordance with the Supplier Contract or this
> Standard, destroyed (including deletion); altered (including
> corruption); or
>
> **B.** lost, accessed by unauthorised personnel, transferred, or
> (without appropriate authorisation) disclosed while it is in the
> possession or under the control of the Supplier or any of the Supplier
> Personnel.

b)  comply with this Standard and any additional policies and standards
    as agreed with Community Fibre to be applicable to the access and
    use of Community Fibre Data.

c)  maintain security control over its technology and physical storage
    infrastructure (by using tools that include firewalls, encryption,
    authentication Services and swipe access);

d)  ensure that:

<!-- -->

i.  all Community Fibre Data is logically segregated and where
    appropriate, physically segregated, in a separate area from all
    non-Community Fibre Data and information held by the Supplier,
    either for itself or on behalf of its other customers or suppliers;

ii. the latest copies of its relevant information security and privacy
    policies and any other information security standards, procedures,
    documented requirements and any supporting evidence demonstrating
    the operation of such policies, standards, procedures and
    requirements are made available to Community Fibre, free of charge,
    at Community Fibre's reasonable request;

<!-- -->

e)  notify Community Fibre of any material changes to the Supplier's
    information security policies, standards, procedures and documented
    requirements and provide copies of the same free of charge, at
    Community Fibre's reasonable request; and

f)  detect, respond and recover Services and Community Fibre Data as the
    result of a security incident occurring, including reporting it to
    Community Fibre as described in section 8 of this Standard.

    1.  In relation to this Standard:

<!-- -->

a)  Community Fibre will be entitled, upon giving notice to the
    Supplier, to carry out or engage an independent organisation of its
    choice to carry out, security audits and tests as it may deem
    necessary in relation to the Supplier's compliance with its
    obligations under this Standard, Good Industry Practice, or as a
    result of an investigation; and

b)  the Supplier will co-operate and facilitate any such audit required
    by Community Fibre of the Supplier's security measures as they apply
    to the Services.

    1.  Where any audit or test reveals any actual or potential security
        or control failure or weakness, the Supplier will promptly, and
        no later than one month after being informed of identified
        risks, notify Community Fibre of any plans to remediate its
        security controls in order to correct such failure or weakness.

    2.  Where such changes to the security controls are to address
        non-compliance with the Supplier's security obligations under
        this Standard, such changes will be at no additional cost to
        Community Fibre.

    3.  The Supplier must notify Community Fibre when:

<!-- -->

a)  it is acquired by another company, or when it acquires or merges
    with another company;

b)  any changes to the production of Hardware or Software supplied to
    Community Fibre occur or are planned which will or may result in
    such Hardware or Software being produced in a different country or
    region; or

c)  any changes to the services being delivered will or may result in
    Community Fibre or customer Data being transferred to, accessed from
    or processed in a different country or region.

    1.  This Standard will be reviewed on an annual basis, or as
        required, to ensure it reflects current Community Fibre working
        practices, industry standards and legislative developments.

### Organisation Security {#organisation-security .Heading-3_Green}

1.  The Supplier will:

<!-- -->

a)  maintain full compliance with:

<!-- -->

i.  a recognized information security management standard for the scope
    of the Services provided to Community Fibre and, where appropriate,
    will provide relevant documentation and confirm the scope of the
    services covered by their certification at Community Fibre's
    request.

ii. NICC ND 1643 where the Services involve interconnecting with the
    Community Fibre network.

iii. Good Industry Practice.

<!-- -->

b)  establish any critical functions and dependencies for the delivery
    of Services and communicate these to Community Fibre in the format
    of supplied documentation ahead of delivery commencing.

c)  document the flows of data involved in the service to be delivered
    to Community Fibre including the type and criticality of data moving
    between internal system components and from internal to external
    system components and share this documentation at request.

d)  ensure that:

<!-- -->

i.  all information security responsibilities are defined, allocated and
    documented within their organisation; (ii) appropriate information
    security training and awareness is conducted for all personnel
    (permanent, contractors and agency) upon hire, following a security
    incident and annually thereafter;

ii. it has an information security policy in place which is reviewed
    annually and communicated to all personnel (permanent, contractors
    and agency) who read and agree to it;

iii. it has an up-to-date privacy policy detailing its handling and
     classification of data which is reviewed annually and communicated
     to all personnel (permanent, contractors and agency) who read and
     agree to it; and

iv. its legal and regulatory requirements are understood and managed.

<!-- -->

e)  on request, require personnel with access to any Community Fibre
    Data, Community Fibre Systems or Community Fibre premises to
    complete Community Fibre's own information security training.

f)  support Community Fibre with the monitoring and performance of the
    security requirements set out in this document.

g)  keep all records relating to compliance with the security
    requirements for at least one year after expiry or earlier
    termination of the Supplier Contract.

h)  conduct appropriate security pre-employment screening and background
    checks for all personnel (including for example, right to work,
    employee references, criminal records, financial); and

i)  regularly repeat background checks for contractors and personnel who
    represent a very high risk of security breach or fraud.

    1.  Community Fibre will also assess any potential detriment to
        security of the Community Fibre Network. Community Fibre expects
        the Supplier to be transparent and open about their security
        practices and to support responsible, independent security
        researchers. For example, through engagement such as responding
        to, triaging and investigating responsibly disclosed issues.
        (TSA)

### Risk Management {#risk-management .Heading-3_Green}

        measures to identify and reduce risk of security compromise:

<!-- -->

b)  that may arise from any other contract for the supply, provision or
    making available of any hardware or software, services or facilities
    in connection with the provision of a service, including public

    1.  The Supplier must have formally documented information security

        place, which are appropriate and demonstrably followed, for the

    2.  In relation to the Services, the Supplier will, with agreement

        critical to provide the Services, or which has access to
        Community Fibre Data, and ensure that they comply with the
        requirements of this Standard or requirements not less stringent
        than this.

2.  

3.  

4.  1.  Where a Supplier is given access to the Community Fibre Network
        via accounts created by Community Fibre:

<!-- -->

a)  These must have access restricted to the Data, applications and
    System(s) that are required to be accessed; and

b)  the Supplier must follow Community Fibre 'joiner, mover and leaver'

    1.  Where, access to any Community Fibre System is given to Supplier

        to access control the Supplier will as a minimum:

<!-- -->

    for the removal of unexpected leavers through an emergency
    procedure.

b)  only permit access to specific individual Supplier Personnel who
    have a clearly defined requirement for access, and not to groups of
    individuals.

c)  grant, monitor and revoke privileges only through a clear chain
    authority and in a timely manner.

d)  ensure that:

<!-- -->

i.  access to any system source code is restricted to only those
    required to have access; and

ii. allocation and use of privileged access rights are restricted and
    controlled.

<!-- -->

e)  conduct a review of logical access control every six months to
    ensure that all staff have the correct access for their current
    role; and

f)  review privileged access on not less than a quarterly basis.

    1.  On all Supplier Systems which store, process or have the
        capability to access Community Fibre Data, in relation to access
        control, the Supplier will:

<!-- -->

a)  immediately disable or delete the User IDs of those individuals who
    are no longer authorised to access any Community Fibre System;

b)  ensure that Community Fibre Data is at all times protected by System
    access controls to ensure that it is not accessed, modified, or
    deleted by unauthorised personnel;

c)  ensure that all Supplier Personnel have unique User IDs allocated to
    them; and

d)  establish password control Systems that ensure that passwords
    allocated conform to standards in accordance with Good Industry
    Practice and are not able to be used by unauthorised parties.

    1.  The Supplier will ensure that physical access to Community Fibre

        appropriately, including:

<!-- -->

    in an area which can only be accessed by authorised Supplier
    Personnel.

b)  laptops and other computers issued by Community Fibre to be kept
    secure by the individual to whom they are issued, and returned to
    Community Fibre when no longer required; and

c)  any removable media containing Community Fibre Data to be encrypted
    and kept locked when not in use.

5.  1.  The Supplier will:

<!-- -->

a)  ensure:

<!-- -->

i.  that in relation to Community Fibre Data appropriate technical and

    processing, accidental damage, or destruction loss.

ii. all Community Fibre Data in transit and at rest is always protected
    using appropriate technical and operational controls.

<!-- -->

b)  arrange for penetration testing - by a suitably qualified person --

c)  Ensure that any assets that hold, store, or process any Community

<!-- -->

i.  built to a defined template through cloning, use of a 'gold build',

ii. recorded on an asset register which is regularly updated and

<!-- -->

d)  afford the same level of protection for printed output e.g.,

<!-- -->

### Protective Technology  {#protective-technology .Heading-3_Green}

6.  1.  If the Supplier controls the administration and operation of any
        specific environments and networks required for the performance
        of the Services and processing of any Community Fibre Data,
        including any Third-Party Services managed by the Supplier, it
        will:

<!-- -->

a)  in relation to cryptography:

<!-- -->

i.  only send Community Fibre Data via communications networks external
    to the Community Fibre network in an encrypted form;

ii. store Community Fibre Data in an encrypted form when at rest;

iii. ensure that, when cryptography is used, only current industry
     standard algorithms are used and, products which have received
     appropriate accreditations in accordance with Good Industry
     Practice;

iv. strictly limit access to cryptographic keys to those who have a need
    to know, and ensure that any master keys cannot be accessed by any
    one individual;

v.  ensure that the information protected by encryption is transmitted
    over a different communication channel than the keys used to govern
    the cryptographic process, or use a standard and approved key
    exchange protocol;

vi. ensure that cryptographic keys and related keying materials used in
    the process are not stored anywhere in unencrypted form; and

vii. ensure that whenever cryptography is used, the sole readable
     version of Community Fibre Data is not deleted until receipt and
     decryption of the encrypted file has been confirmed.

<!-- -->

b)  in relation to protection from malicious Software:

<!-- -->

i.  implement controls to prevent and detect the introduction of
    malicious Software and viruses into any Systems holding Community
    Fibre Data or used by Community Fibre or the Supplier in relation to
    the Services; and

ii. implement commercial grade anti-virus software and ensure it is kept
    up to date.

<!-- -->

c)  in relation to network security controls:

<!-- -->

i.  establish appropriate controls to ensure the security of Community
    Fibre Data in networks, and the protection of connected Services
    from unauthorised access;

ii. implement controls to safeguard the integrity and confidentiality of
    Community Fibre Data passing over public networks, and to protect
    the connected Systems;

iii. implement controls to protect against data leakage;

iv. authenticate connections to remote computer Systems; and

v.  configure firewalls and gateway devices to allow only authorised
    traffic to flow through them.

<!-- -->

d)  in relation to development processes:

<!-- -->

i.  carry out Software/application development and testing with
    Community Fibre test Data or anonymised production Community Fibre
    Data where that test data has been anonymised or pseudonymised to
    the extent that it is considered so under the terms of the relevant
    Data Protection Laws to ensure it cannot be traced back to a living
    individual, unless otherwise agreed in writing by Community Fibre;

ii. ensure separate transactions are used for testing or training
    purposes from transactions used for production processing;

iii. where Systems are operated by the Supplier, ensure that access to
     compilers, assemblers, editors, and other general-purpose utilities
     within the test environment is restricted; and

iv. provide assurances/warranties that none of the Software contains
    undocumented features or hidden mechanisms that could be used to
    compromise the Software\'s security.

<!-- -->

e)  in relation to Software developed specifically for Community Fibre,
    the Supplier must ensure:

<!-- -->

i.  the Software provider adheres to secure code production guidelines,
    which must include code inspection to verify the absence of security
    weaknesses;

ii. for "off-the shelf" Software, the Supplier must satisfy itself that
    any Software licence adequately describes the Software capabilities,
    which must be validated by the Supplier's own security testing at
    the Supplier's own cost;

iii. to obtain written confirmation from any third-party providing
     Software in relation to the Services that the Software will not
     require the modification or renewal of existing Community Fibre
     computer security Systems; and

iv. to not utilise any free or downloaded code not controlled by the
    Supplier (e.g., shared code-banks, repositories from on-line sources
    that may harbour unknown procedures, malware, or access rights).

### Security Monitoring {#security-monitoring .Heading-3_Green}

7.  1.  If requested by Community Fibre in relation to the Services
        provided, the Supplier will provide assurance that newly
        identified vulnerabilities are mitigated within defined
        timescales no longer than those outlined in Appendix E.
        Compliance must be demonstrated using supporting evidence at
        Community Fibre's request.

    2.  All assets processing Community Fibre Data must be monitored
        regularly to identify security events/ incidents and verify the
        effectiveness of protective measures.

    3.  In relation to Community Fibre Data, all system, application,
        and user access must be logged and monitored, and logs must be:

<!-- -->

a)  stored centrally, away from source and protected from unauthorised
    access, where central storage of logs is not possible, logs must be
    securely stored locally;

b)  maintained with sufficient quality to be used as admissible evidence
    during forensic investigation;

c)  reviewed regularly to ensure the admissibility of evidence as part
    of any forensic investigations; and

    1.  protected against tampering and unauthorised access. The
        supplier must ensure that appropriate technical controls are in
        place to prevent the accidental or malicious exfiltration of
        Community Fibre Data via external electronic communication
        channels such as USB mass storage devices, email, and web-based
        file transfer sites e.g., Data Loss Prevention, Web filtering
        and email filtering.

### Incident Management  {#incident-management .Heading-3_Green}

1.  The Supplier will ensure that Supplier Personnel are aware of their
    roles in identifying, reporting, and responding to information
    security events or incidents.

2.  If the Supplier becomes aware of any actual, potential, or attempted
    Breach of Security; or any breach of its obligations under this
    Third-Party Security Standard, it will immediately:

<!-- -->

a)  notify Community Fibre incident management team and the relevant
    business partner at Community Fibre of the actual or suspected
    breaches of security of its obligations under this Third-Party
    Security Standard and report on the potential effect of such breach
    to Community Fibre as soon as possible (and, in any event, within 24
    hours);

b)  identify a single point of contact for Community Fibre to liaise
    with during the management of the security incident;

c)  take immediate steps to contain any breach to prevent further impact
    as appropriate;

d)  take all reasonable steps necessary, as approved by Community Fibre,
    to remedy such breach (to the extent that it is capable of
    remediation) or protect Community Fibre Data against any such
    potential or attempted breach or threat, promptly investigate such
    breach and provide Community Fibre with regular updates throughout;

e)  advise Community Fibre of any Supplier Personnel implicated in the
    incident and take appropriate disciplinary action, including
    suspension if required, upon Community Fibre's request;

f)  promptly take measures to ensure that such breach does not occur
    again and provide a written report to Community Fibre detailing the
    measures taken;

g)  document the response to the matter and finding of the investigation
    into such breach and issue this to Community Fibre as soon as
    practical whilst continuing to provide investigation and remediation
    action point updates to Community Fibre, until such time they are
    completed to the satisfaction of Community Fibre;

h)  where criminal activity is suspected, or a breach of contractual or
    regulatory requirements and, if requested by Community Fibre:

<!-- -->

i.  engage an independent third-party approved by Community Fibre to
    gather legally admissible evidence or contact the law enforcement
    agencies;

ii. allow Community Fibre access to conduct its own investigations or
    work with the engaged third-party; and

iii. ensure all communications with third parties or the media are
     agreed in writing by Community Fibre before release.

     1.  Any costs that Community Fibre incurs that are associated with
         any Breach of Security response or investigation will be

### Appendix G - Applicable to Telecommunications (Security) Act 2021 {#appendix-g---applicable-to-telecommunications-security-act-2021 .Heading-3_Green}

### Introduction

1.  The following requirements apply to all Suppliers considered to be
    in scope of the Telecommunications Security Act:

<!-- -->

a)  Where the Supplier has been engaged to support a network, equipment,
    service or facilities (including equipment vendors, carriers, 3rd
    party administrators, transit providers, SaaS providers, data
    centres and colocation providers) on behalf of Community Fibre, the
    Supplier warrants it shall ensure compliance with the
    Telecommunications (Security) Act 2021 and associated regulations,
    measures and oversight in its own right; (M2.06)

b)  The Supplier acknowledges and permits that in some circumstances
    Community Fibre will wish to share details of security issues with
    other appropriate parties to support the identification and
    reduction of the risks of security compromises occurring across
    industry stakeholders in relation to the public electronic
    communications network or public electronic communications service
    as a result of things done or omitted by on their behalf (M10.46)
    and to improve and maintain compliance with legal, regulatory and
    contractual obligations; and

c)  **Where security failings are not resolved within a reasonable time
    frame Community Fibre shall be entitled to deem this a material
    breach of obligation, not capable of** **remedy and free to exit any
    and all contracts with the Supplier without penalty. (M10.15)**

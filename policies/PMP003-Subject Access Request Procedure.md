### Scope {#scope .Heading-3_Green}

All personal data processed by Community Fibre is within the scope of
this procedure.

Data subjects are entitled to obtain the following:

- Confirmation as to whether Community Fibre is processing any personal
  data about that individual.

- Access to their personal data.

- Any related information.

### Responsibilities {#responsibilities .Heading-3_Green}

1.  

2.  1.  The Data Protection Officer is responsible for the application
        and effective working of this procedure, and for reporting to
        the information owner Chief Information Officer on subject
        access requests (SARs).

    2.  The Data Protection Officer is responsible for handling all
        SARs.

### Procedure {#procedure .Heading-3_Green}

1.  

2.  

3.  1.  SARs are made using the Subject Access Request Record.

    2.  The data subject provides Community Fibre with evidence of their
        identity, in the form of a current passport/driving license, and
        the signature on the identity must be cross-checked to that on
        the application form (Subject Access Request Record).

    3.  The data subject identifies the specific set of data held by
        Community Fibre on their SAR. The data subject can request all
        data held on them.

    4.  Community Fibre records the date that the identification checks
        were conducted, and the specification of the data sought.

    5.  Community Fibre provides the requested information to the data
        subject within one month from this recorded date.

    6.  Once received, the SAR application is immediately forwarded to
        the Data Protection Officer, who will ensure that the requested
        data is collected within the specified time frame in Clause 3.5
        above. Collection entails:

        1.  Collecting the data specified by the data subject.

        2.  Searching all databases and all relevant filing systems
            (manual files) in Community Fibre, including all backup and
            archived files (computerised or manual) and all email
            folders and archives. The Data Protection Officer maintains
            a data map that identifies where all data in Community Fibre
            is stored in ISMS.online

    7.  The Data Protection Officer maintains a record of requests for
        data and of its receipt, including dates in ISMS.online.

    8.  The Data Protection Officer reviews SARs from a child. Before
        responding to a SAR of the child data subject, the Data
        Protection Officer considers their ability to make the request
        by adequately explaining any implications of sharing their
        personal data.

    9.  The Data Protection Officer reviews all documents that have been
        provided to identify whether any third parties are present in
        it, and either removes the identifying third-party information
        from the documentation or obtains written consent from the third
        party for their identity to be revealed.

    10. If any of the requested data is being held or processed under
        one of the following exemptions, it does not have to be
        provided:

- National security

- Crime and taxation

- Health

- Regulatory activity

- Publicly available information

- Corporate finance

- Confidential references

- Management forecasts

- Legal advice and proceedings

  1.  If a data subject requests Community Fibre to provide them with
      the personal data stored by the controller/processor, then
      Community Fibre will provide the data subject with the requested
      information in electronic format, unless otherwise specified. All
      the items provided to the data subject are listed in Records of
      Processing Activity (ROPA) hosted in ISMS.online, which shows the
      data subject's name and the date on which the information is
      delivered to the data subject.

  2.  If a data subject requests what personal data is being processed,
      then Community Fibre provides the data subject with the following
      information:

      1.  Purpose of the processing.

      2.  Categories of personal data.

      3.  Recipient(s) of the information, including recipients in third
          countries or international organisations.

      4.  How long the personal data will be stored.

      5.  The data subject's right to request rectification or erasure,
          restriction, or objection, relative to their personal data
          being processed.

      6.  Community Fibre removes personal data from systems and
          processing operations as soon as a request for erasure has
          been submitted by the data subject.

      7.  Community Fibre contacts and communicates via email with other
          organisations, where the personal data of the data subject is
          being processed, to cease processing information at the
          request of the data subject.

      8.  Community Fibre takes appropriate measures without undue delay
          if the data subject has withdrawn consent or objects to the
          processing of their personal data in whole or part; or
          Community Fibre is no longer under legal obligation; and/or
          the personal data has been unlawfully processed.

      9.  Inform the data subject of their right to lodge a complaint
          with the supervisory authority and a method to do so.

      10. Information on the source of the personal data if it has not
          been collected from the data subject.

      11. Information about any automated decision-making.

      12. If and where personal data has been transferred and
          information on any safeguards in place.

  3.  Community Fibre uses the following electronic formats to respond
      to SARs: common machine-readable formats including Microsoft
      Office documents, Adobe PDF's, and .mp3 audio files.

- Information Security Management System BS EN ISO/IEC 27001:2022 -- A.6
  People Controls

### Document Objective {#document-objective .Heading-3_Green}

Community Fibre Limited has a responsibility to ensure that employees,
contractors, and temporary staff are properly briefed on their
information security roles and responsibilities. The objective for this
document is to:

- To ensure that employees and contractors understand their
  responsibilities and are suitable for the roles for which they are
  considered.

- To ensure that employees and contractors are aware of and fulfil their
  information security responsibilities.

- To protect the organisation's interests as part of the process of
  changing or terminating employment.

### Scope {#scope .Heading-3_Green}

Community Fibre Limited employs staff, contractors, and agency staff.
This policy applies to all business functions where information is used
or processed, and includes functions supplied on its behalf by external
contractors or suppliers. This policy is applicable to all data and
information and to all people accessing Community Fibre Limited data and
information from any location, regardless of the method used.

### Policy {#policy .Heading-3_Green}

1.  All Community Fibre Limited employees, whether holding a temporary,
    fixed term or open contract, must comply with the Information
    Security Policy. [ISP003-Information Security
    Policy.pdf](https://communityfibre.sharepoint.com/:b:/r/sites/isms/Shared%20Documents/General/ISMS%20Policy%20Documents/Approved/ISP003-Information%20Security%20Policy.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=gFOYPC)

2.  All employees are informed that they are required to follow
    Community Fibre Limited policies relating to data protection and
    information security when they receive their terms and conditions of
    employment.

3.  Violation of Community Fibre Limited's data protection or
    information security policies may lead to disciplinary action.

4.  Depending on the information security requirements, the Community
    Fibre Limited may make additional background checks or conduct
    additional tests during the recruitment process to assess the
    suitability of candidates for a role.

5.  All employees must undertake information security and data
    protection training during their induction to raise their awareness
    of the risks and issues associated with handling Community Fibre
    Limited information, and the appropriate safeguards. This training
    will be made available through Community Fibre Limited's training
    platform.

6.  All employees will be informed of the need to report information
    security and data protection incidents quickly and of the
    appropriate method for doing so. Periodic reminders will be issued
    to all employees.

7.  All employees must maintain their knowledge and skills in relation
    to information security throughout their employment at the Community
    Fibre Limited, undertaking training as required.

8.  At the request of the Head of Human Resources (or equivalent senior
    manager), access to information or IT systems may be removed.

9.  Employees who leave the organisation will have their access
    privileges terminated in line with the Access Control Policy.
    [ISP034-Access Control
    Policy.pdf](https://communityfibre.sharepoint.com/:b:/r/sites/isms/Shared%20Documents/General/ISMS%20Policy%20Documents/Approved/ISP034-Access%20Control%20Policy.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=oObr8j)

10. On leaving the Community Fibre Limited, employees must return all
    information assets and equipment belonging to Community Fibre
    Limited.

### Responsibilities {#responsibilities .Heading-3_Green}

1.  All information users are responsible for protecting and ensuring
    the security of the information to which they have access.

2.  Community Fibre Limited senior management, heads of functions and
    line managers are responsible for ensuring that all information in
    their area is managed in conformance with this policy.

3.  Employees, agency staff or contractors who act in breach of this
    policy, or who do not act to implement it, may be subject to
    disciplinary procedures or other appropriate sanctions.

### Human resource security {#human-resource-security .Heading-3_Green}

#### 7.1 Prior to employment {#prior-to-employment .Heading-4_Purple}

#### 7.1.1 Screening {#screening .Heading-4_Purple}

Community Fibre Limited carries out background checks on all candidates
for employment. This includes independent identity check (passport or
similar) and confirmation of claimed academic and professional
qualifications.

#### 7.1.2 Terms and conditions of employment {#terms-and-conditions-of-employment .Heading-4_Purple}

As part of their terms and conditions of employment all employees,
contractor and third-party users who are given access to sensitive
information, should sign a confidentiality or nondisclosure agreement
prior to being given access to information processing systems or
facilities. (Agreements should be in place with suppliers that apply to
all their staff or staff individually should accept Community Fibre
Limited confidentiality and nondisclosure agreement.)

#### 7.2 During employment {#during-employment .Heading-4_Purple}

#### 7.2.1 Management responsibilities {#management-responsibilities .Heading-4_Purple}

Management shall ensure that employees:

- Complete the mandatory online learning.

- Are made aware of and follow all relevant information security
  policies.

- Are trained appropriately to the use of any IT System.

#### 7.2.2 Information security awareness, education, and training {#information-security-awareness-education-and-training .Heading-4_Purple}

All employees shall receive a formal induction process designed to
introduce the organisation's security policies and expectations prior to
being provided with access to information or services. Training and
awareness will be delivered using a combination of methods including:

- Companywide communications (i.e., email) campaigns

- BambooHR [Files
  (bamboohr.com)](https://communityfibre.bamboohr.com/files/)

- Employee Intranet (SharePoint document library at [ISMS Policies -
  Approved](https://communityfibre.sharepoint.com/:f:/r/sites/isms/Shared%20Documents/General/ISMS%20Policy%20Documents/Approved?csf=1&web=1&e=fxM42D))

- Awareness training at induction and then at set intervals

#### 7.2.3 Disciplinary process {#disciplinary-process .Heading-4_Purple}

The Community Fibre Limited disciplinary policy is available in the
Employee Handbook on BambooHR at [[Files
(bamboohr.com)]{.underline}](https://communityfibre.bamboohr.com/files/).

#### 7.3 Termination and change of employment. {#termination-and-change-of-employment. .Heading-4_Purple}

Information security responsibilities and duties as described in the
contract of employment remain valid after termination or change of
employment shall be defined, communicated to the employee or contractor,
and enforced.

### General Information {#general-information .Heading-3_Green}

### Document Objective {#document-objective .Heading-3_Purple}

This User Registration Policy outlines the requirements for registering
and de-registering users at Community Fibre Limited, ensuring secure
onboarding and offboarding to protect information assets. It supports
the overarching [ISP010-Access Control
Policy](https://communityfibre.sharepoint.com/:b:/r/sites/isms/Shared%20Documents/General/ISMS%20Policy%20Documents/Approved/ISP010-Access%20Control%20Policy.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=XxV42z)
by focusing on user registration and de-registration processes and
complements other supporting policies such as the[ISP011 -- Information
Access Rights
Policy](https://communityfibre.sharepoint.com/:b:/r/sites/isms/Shared%20Documents/General/ISMS%20Policy%20Documents/Approved/ISP012-Identity%20Management%20Policy.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=12e4Yd)
, [ISP040-Password Management
Policy](https://communityfibre.sharepoint.com/:b:/r/sites/isms/Shared%20Documents/General/ISMS%20Policy%20Documents/Approved/ISP040-Password%20Management%20Policy.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=2Rdg3c),
[ISP012-Identity Management
Policy](https://communityfibre.sharepoint.com/:b:/r/sites/isms/Shared%20Documents/General/ISMS%20Policy%20Documents/Approved/ISP012-Identity%20Management%20Policy.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=4xDgyR),
and [ISP013 -- Privileged Access Rights
Policy](https://communityfibre.sharepoint.com/:b:/r/sites/isms/Shared%20Documents/General/ISMS%20Policy%20Documents/Approved/ISP011%20%E2%80%93%20Information%20Access%20Rights%20Policy.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=dTSIrD).

This policy ensures alignment with business needs, information security
risks, and regulatory requirements, including the Electronic
Communications (Security Measures) Regulations 2022 and its associated
Code of Practice.

**This document meets the following clauses:**

- Information Security Management System ISO/IEC 27001:2022 -- A.5.16:
  Identity management (user registration aspects)

- The Electronic Communications (Security Measures) Regulations 2022 --
  Regulation 8: Access controls for security-critical functions,
  including unique credentials and verification

### Policy {#policy .Heading-3_Green}

### Scope and principles

This policy applies to the registration and de-registration of all
users, including employees, contractors, third parties, and
system/service accounts, who require access to Community Fibre\'s
information assets, networks, systems, and services. It covers the full
user lifecycle and aligns with joiners, movers, and leavers processes.

Key principles:

- Every user must be assigned a unique identifier for accountability.

- Registration controls are proportionate to the level of access and
  asset sensitivity (per [ISP037-Information Classification and Handling
  Policy](https://communityfibre.sharepoint.com/:b:/r/sites/isms/Shared%20Documents/General/ISMS%20Policy%20Documents/Approved/ISP037-Information%20Classification%20and%20Handling%20Policy.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=6FuEDR)).

- Initial access is minimal, based on role and need-to-know.

- Use automation for efficient, error-free registration
  ([ISP012-Identity Management
  Policy](https://communityfibre.sharepoint.com/:b:/r/sites/isms/Shared%20Documents/General/ISMS%20Policy%20Documents/Approved/ISP044-Secure%20Disposal%20Policy.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=JI1TaH)).

- Ensure immediate removal of access upon departure or change to prevent
  unauthorised use.

- All processes are auditable and aligned with legal and regulatory
  obligations.

User registration is managed by HR, IT Service Desk, and Security
Operations, with oversight from the ISMS Board.

### User registration process

- Registration is initiated by a formal request from an authorised
  manager or HR for employees, or via contracts for contractors/third
  parties.

- The process includes:

  - Obtaining full name, role, contact information, and justification
    for access.

  - Assignment of a unique user ID, linked to the individual\'s identity
    ([ISP012-Identity Management
    Policy](https://communityfibre.sharepoint.com/:b:/r/sites/isms/Shared%20Documents/General/ISMS%20Policy%20Documents/Approved/ISP044-Secure%20Disposal%20Policy.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=JI1TaH)).

  - Initial access provisioning based on RBAC, aligned with job role and
    least privilege ([ISP011 -- Information Access Rights
    Policy](https://communityfibre.sharepoint.com/:b:/r/sites/isms/Shared%20Documents/General/ISMS%20Policy%20Documents/Approved/ISP012-Identity%20Management%20Policy.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=12e4Yd)).

  - Issuance of authentication information, such as temporary passwords
    or MFA setup ([ISP040-Password Management
    Policy](https://communityfibre.sharepoint.com/:b:/r/sites/isms/Shared%20Documents/General/ISMS%20Policy%20Documents/Approved/ISP040-Password%20Management%20Policy.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=bNDyRE)).

  - For privileged access, additional approvals and controls apply (per
    [ISP013 -- Privileged Access Rights
    Policy](https://communityfibre.sharepoint.com/:b:/r/sites/isms/Shared%20Documents/General/ISMS%20Policy%20Documents/Approved/ISP011%20%E2%80%93%20Information%20Access%20Rights%20Policy.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=dTSIrD)).

- Users must acknowledge acceptance of policies (e.g.,
  [ISP030-Acceptable Use
  Policy.pdf](https://communityfibre.sharepoint.com/:b:/r/sites/isms/Shared%20Documents/General/ISMS%20Policy%20Documents/Approved/ISP030-Acceptable%20Use%20Policy.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=haOMFr),
  Code of Conduct) and complete security awareness training within a
  reasonable timeframe after activation.

- Automated tools (e.g., integration with Microsoft Entra ID) are used
  for provisioning to ensure consistency and speed.

### Verification and authentication

- Identity verification is required prior to registration:

  - HR verifies via background checks, ID documents, and right-to-work
    confirmation.

  - Contracts, NDAs/DPAs, and supplier risk assessments must be
    completed.

  - Basic for Public/Confidential assets; enhanced (e.g., security
    vetting) for Strictly Confidential or privileged access.

- Authentication setup includes:

  - Unique, non-guessable credentials; no defaults.

  - MFA enablement for remote or sensitive access.

  - Risk-based checks (e.g., behavioural analytics) during initial
    login.

- Temporary credentials must be changed immediately upon first use.

### User de-registration process

- De-registration is triggered immediately by HR notifications (e.g.,
  termination) or security incidents.

- The process includes:

  - Revocation of all access rights and credentials ([ISP011 --
    Information Access Rights
    Policy](https://communityfibre.sharepoint.com/:b:/r/sites/isms/Shared%20Documents/General/ISMS%20Policy%20Documents/Approved/ISP012-Identity%20Management%20Policy.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=12e4Yd)).

  - Disablement or deletion of user IDs ([ISP012-Identity Management
    Policy](https://communityfibre.sharepoint.com/:b:/r/sites/isms/Shared%20Documents/General/ISMS%20Policy%20Documents/Approved/ISP044-Secure%20Disposal%20Policy.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=JI1TaH)).

  - Return or secure disposal of company assets (e.g., devices, badges).

  - Automated de-provisioning where integrated; manual steps logged and
    confirmed.

- Dormant accounts (inactive for 90 days) are automatically disabled and
  reviewed for permanent removal.

- Post-de-registration audits ensure no residual access remains.

### Third-party and contractor registration

- Third-party users follow the same processes but with additional
  controls:

  - Registration requires NDA and approved contracts and specifying
    access scope, duration, and monitoring.

  - Access is time-bound and limited to specific systems/assets.

  - Remote access restricted to jump boxes or managed devices; UK-based
    where possible.

  - Regular reviews (e.g., quarterly) to confirm ongoing need.

- Risks from third parties are assessed and mitigated (e.g., via DPAs
  for PII handling).v

### Monitoring and review

- All registration and de-registration activities are logged by the IT
  Service Desk.

- Periodic reviews (at least every 6 months) validate active users
  against business needs.

**\**

### Regulatory requirements

- User registration for security-critical functions ensures unique
  credentials, verification, and segregation (Regulation 8(2) (b-c),
  8(5)).

- Remote access is restricted to managed devices (Regulation 4(5)).

- Third-party risks are assessed (Regulation 11(b)), with logging of
  registrations (Code of Practice para 3.4-3.7).

- Training includes competency on access controls (Regulation 13(2)(a)).

    General Information¶

    Document Objective¶

    This Access Control Policy provides the overarching framework for managing access to Community Fibre's information assets, networks, systems, and facilities. It ensures access is appropriate, proportionate to risks, and aligned with business and compliance requirements.

    This policy serves as the primary reference for access controls within the ISMS, supported by more detailed policy documents.

    This document meets the following clauses:

    • ISO/IEC 27001:2022 -- A.5.15: Access control

    • NCSC Cyber Assessment Framework (CAF) v3.1 - B2 Identity and Access Control

    • The Electronic Communications(Security Measures) Regulations 2022 -- Access controls for security-critical functions, including least privilege, unique credentials, and monitoring

    Supporting policies and documents¶

    Policy¶

    Scope and principles¶

    This policy applies to all access, physical and logical, to Community Fibre\'s information assets, networks, systems, services, and facilities, including those managed by employees, contractors, third parties, and automated processes. It covers the full lifecycle of access rights, ensuring alignment with business needs, information security risks, and regulatory requirements, including the Telecommunications (Security) Act 2021 and its accompanying Code of Practice.

    Topic-specific approach¶

    Access controls are implemented based on the sensitivity of assets (per ISP037 -- Information Classification and Handling Policy) and risk assessments. This includes physical controls for facilities and logical controls for systems, with a focus on security-critical functions in telecom infrastructure.

    Supporting policies¶

    Detailed procedures are outlined in supporting documents:

    1. Information Access Rights Policy (ISP011): Outlines the requirements for granting access rights to various information assets based on employees\' roles and responsibilities. It aims to establish consistency between the data and systems to which access is given and the type of access rights assigned.

    2. Password Management Policy (ISP040): Mandates the use of strong, unique passwords for each user, based on up-to-date recognised best practice, including the use of Multi-Factor Authentication, guidance for the creation of secure passwords and secure handling guidelines.

    3. Identity Management Policy (ISP012): Governs the creation, maintenance, and revocation of user identities within our systems. It also includes provisions for role-based access control (RBAC) to ensure authorised access privileges are granted based on predefined job functions and responsibilities.

    4. Privileged Access Rights Policy (ISP042): Defines the process for granting, monitoring, and auditing access privileges and Privileged Access Workstations (PAWs) for users with privileged access rights. We closely monitor users with privileged access to prevent any compromise of our Access Control function.

    5. User Registration Policy (ISP043): Sets guidelines for onboarding and offboarding employees and other entities. When granting access, we follow a structured, formal access request and authorisation process, ensuring documented approval from the appropriate personnel.

    6. Physical Security Standard (ISD006): Set guidelines for physical entry and perimeter controls to prevent unauthorised access, damage, or interference with information assets and infrastructure.

    Logging & Monitoring¶

    There is a duty to implement measures to monitor security-critical functions in public electronic communications networks or services, to detect any security breaches. (The Electronic Communications (Security Measures) Regulations 2022 Reg 6(2)(a)).

    All access activities are logged and monitored:

    • Logical access logs include authentication attempts, changes, and anomalies.

    • Physical access logs track entry/exit.

    • Account and assigned role or permissions reviews occur every 6 months.

    • Incidents are investigated by Security Operations.

    Training & Awareness¶

    All users receive training on access controls upon onboarding and annually:

    • There is a duty to ensure competence of responsible persons (The Electronic Communications (Security Measures) Regulations 2022 Reg 13(2)(a)).

    • Specialised training for privileged users.

    Consequences of non-compliance with this policy¶

    Any non-compliance with or breach of policy may lead to investigation and action in line with the organisational Disciplinary Policy (CFL Employee Handbook 2023 v.3).

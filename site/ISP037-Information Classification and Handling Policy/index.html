    General Information¶

    The Data Classification and Handling Policy describes the classifications and protective measures that apply to all Community Fibre Limited information and documentation in both electronic and hardcopy forms.

    This document meets the following clauses:

    • Information Security Management System BS EN ISO/IEC 27002:2022 -- A.5.12: Classification of information

    The purpose of this control is to ensure identification and understanding of protection needs of information in accordance with its importance to Community Fibre and especially to protect privacy and personally identifiable information (PII).

    We recognise the importance of effective information classification to protect information and associated assets based on their value and importance.

    Our classification policy (below) is based on:

    • Risk assessment;

    • Requirements for the confidentiality, integrity; and availability of information and associated assets;

    • Pragmatic rules to balance security requirements against efficacy of working practices.

    {#section .Heading-3_Green}¶

    Policy¶

    All information and associated assets are classified as follows:

    • Public

    • Confidential

    • Strictly confidential

    4. Customer classification mapping¶

    For information received from customers that is already marked with their classification scheme, we adopt a practical implicit mapping process that avoids the need for remarking internally and then marking back again when sharing back with the customer.

    For example, with customers using the UK Government Classification Scheme (GCS) where we receive information from them the following applies:

    • "Public" maps to "unclassified"

    • "Confidential" maps to "OFFICIAL"

    • "Sensitive" maps to "OFFICIAL-SENSITIVE"

    5. Customer Payment Card Information Handling¶

    As part of our commitment to protecting our customers\' sensitive information, we adhere to the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) for handling cardholder data. The following guidelines must be observed by all employees, contractors, and third parties who interact with, process, store, or transmit cardholder data:

    Limit Data Exposure: Only collect cardholder data when necessary and limit access to this data to only those individuals who need this information to perform their job duties.

    Secure Storage: Do not store sensitive cardholder data (like CVV or CVV2) under any circumstances. Other cardholder data that needs to be stored must be encrypted and protected by appropriate access controls.

    Transmission Security: Encrypt cardholder data whenever it is transmitted across open, public networks.

    Data Disposal: Securely dispose of cardholder data that is no longer needed, in accordance with our data retention policy and the PCI DSS. This includes physical cardholder data and electronic media.

    Monitor Access: All access to cardholder data must be logged and monitored. Regular audits of access logs should be conducted to identify and respond to suspicious activity.

    Incident Response: Any suspected or confirmed security incidents involving cardholder data must be reported immediately to our Security Team.

    Training: All staff members who handle cardholder data must complete PCI DSS training upon hire and annually thereafter.

    6. Consequences of non-compliance with this policy¶

    Any non-compliance with or breach of policy may lead to investigation and action in line with the organisational Disciplinary Policy (Community Fibre - Employee Handbook 2023 - v3).

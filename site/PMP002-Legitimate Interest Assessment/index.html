    Introduction¶

    This Legitimate Interest Policy outlines the framework under which Community Fibre Limited processes personal data based on the legal basis of \"Legitimate Interests\" as per the Data Protection Act 2018 and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

    Scope¶

    This policy applies to all personal data processed by Community Fibre Limited and its subsidiaries, where the legal basis for such processing is legitimate interest.

    Definitions¶

    • Personal Data: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

    • Processing: Any operation performed on personal data, whether automated or not.

    • Legitimate Interests: A legal basis for processing personal data, other than consent, contract, or legal obligation

    Identification of Legitimate Interests¶

    Before processing any personal data based on legitimate interests, [Your Company Name] will:

    • Clearly identify the legitimate interests pursued.

    • Conduct a Legitimate Interests Assessment (LIA) to ensure that these interests are not overridden by the data subject's interests, rights, or freedoms.

    Legitimate Interests Assessment (LIA)¶

    The LIA will include:

    • Purpose Test: Identify the legitimate interest behind the processing.

    • Necessity Test: Ensure that the processing is necessary to achieve the identified interests.

    • Balancing Test: Ensure that the legitimate interests are not overridden by the data subject\'s interests, rights, or freedoms.

    Data Minimization¶

    Community Fibre Limited will only process data that is strictly necessary for the identified legitimate interests.

    Transparency¶

    Data subjects will be informed about the processing activities based on legitimate interests through privacy notices or other appropriate means.

    Data Subject Rights¶

    Data subjects have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Upon receiving an objection, [Your Company Name] will immediately cease processing unless it can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for the processing.

    Record-keeping¶

    All LIAs and related documentation will be kept on record and made available for inspection by regulatory authorities.

    Review¶

    This policy will be reviewed at least annually or whenever there is a significant change in processing activities.¶

    General Information¶

    Document Objective¶

    The process description below outlines the steps involved in managing Joiners, Movers, and Leavers (JML) to ensure compliance with ISO27001 standards and maintain information security integrity.

    This document meets the following clauses:

    • Information Security Management System BS EN ISO/IEC 27002:2017 -- A.6 People Controls

    Starters/Movers/Leavers Process¶

    Setting up a new starter¶

    • Recruitment will inform HR Operations once they have offered a new role and candidate has accepted a job offer.

    • Team Tailor will generate notification to Zinc to send out RTW Checks

    • HR Operations will set up a new account in BambooHR once candidate has accepted the job offer and a start date is agreed.

    • HR Operations will setup the new BambooHR account with the following minimum details:

    • Full Name

    • Full Address

    • Phone Number

    • Email Address

    • Hire Date

    • Employment Status effective date

    • Employment status (FTE, Part Time, etc)

    • Job Title

    • Reporting manager

    • Department

    • Division

    • Location

    • Pay Rate

    • Pay type

    • Pay schedule

    • Once this is completed a "hiring packet" is sent to the new employee via BambooHR "Save and Create New Hire Packet". The employee will confirm their details and add the following to their hiring packet:

    • Employee completes home address, email address and phone number

    • mandatory documents to complete are contract and offer letter

    • The Starter/Leaver tracker document shared with Recruitment and IT is updated so that they are informed of new starters, date of role commencing, and so that IT accounts and equipment is arranged on time.

    New starter reference checking and DBS Checks¶

    • Recruitment will align a candidate in Teamtailor with the necessary Zinc package to carry out further checks.

    • Candidate will be sent a link to complete details for references, Right to Work Checks, Address Checks and DBS checks.

    • HR Operations will obtain at least one reference and have proven attempts that there were attempts to contacts at least one more. This is actioned via Zinc

    • Visa & passport information is verified by Zinc and updated in Bamboo once accepted

    • HR Operations let the recruiter know once all checks have been carried out.

    Movers¶

    • Departmental leaders will send an email to HR Operations to inform them of a staff member moving into another role.

    • HR Operations will send an offer letter as an addendum to the employees existing employment contract outlining necessary changes to conditions of employment.

    • HR Operations will adjust information in BambooHR as reflected in the new offer letter.

    • HR Operations to send a notification of a staff member's role changing to IT service desk.

    • IT service desk to adjust assigned roles and group membership as required.

    Leavers¶

    • Email, including a written resignation from the leaver, where obtainable, is sent to HR Operations, where an Acknowledgement Letter is produced and provided to the upcoming leaver.

    • HR complete IT Leavers form with confirmation of leaver date.

    • HR to inform Payroll of leaver's exit date.

    Payroll to update ADP and company pension etc. with exit date.

    • IT will contact leaver straight away to arrange collection of any relevant company property, e.g., Mobile, laptop etc.

    • IT Service desk to deactivate all accounts on agreed exit date.

    • Payroll will terminate them in ADP and company pension following their final pay.

    Introduction¶

    The purpose of this document is to provide an overview of Community Fibre Limited, the activities it conducts and the ISMS standards of operation it conforms to.

    Community Fibre Limited handles staff, vendor, corporate, customer and customer personnel data and the business must be able to demonstrate that information security has been integrated into processes, and that all information is managed securely.

    Certification to ISO 27001:2022 of the Community Fibre Limited ISMS will provide assurance internally to management and stakeholders, as well as to customer and other interested parties, that its information is managed in a controlled and secure manner, and that Community Fibre Limited is committed to providing a reliable level of service.

    The company is committed to meeting regulatory and compliance obligations, including the UK GDPR/Data Protection Act 2018 and the Telecommunications (Security) Act 2021. Compliance with these regulations ensures that Community Fibre maintains high standards of assurance, resilience, and accountability.

    The ISMS is designed to align with ISO 27001:2022 and any standard adopting the Annex SL structure.

    Overview of the organisation¶

    Founded in 2010, Community Fibre provides high-speed broadband across Greater London and Surrey through its fully owned and operated optical fibre network.

    The company is committed to serving multi and single-dwelling, businesses and increasingly wholesale partners. Community Fibre offers competitive pricing, exceptional customer support, and high-speed, reliable, full-fibre connectivity. Its dedication to customer service has earned it one of the highest Trustpilot ratings among UK ISPs.

    Community Fibre provides:

    • Fibre broadband services to residential and business customers.

    • Fibre network wholesale services to other and often larger UK telecoms businesses.

    • Support and billing services for residential and business customers.

    Purposes¶

    The purpose of the ISMS and information security program is to protect the organisation, network and business infrastructure, and the staff and customer information it collects, processes, and stores.

    This is achieved through the documented policies and procedures, education and awareness initiatives, and appropriate and proportionate technical and organisational controls. The scope of the ISMS encompasses core network services and business systems and operations.

    Community Fibre recognises the critical value and importance of securing the information and network assets that drive our business. The ISMS supports our key strategic aims of:

    Excellent service -- Security continuity (the practice of keeping Community Fibre's data and systems protected and operational, no matter what disruptions hit us) differentiates our services from other organisations, exceeds customer expectations, and enables us to operate effectively in an increasingly regulated environment.

    Network and information asset protection -- Through this ISMS, we aim to minimise risks associated with malicious activities, accidental damage, or disruptions to information assets and network infrastructure, regardless of their form (digital, physical, or otherwise).

    Regulatory and contractual requirements - Drives compliance with relevant statutory regulations, including the Telecommunications (Security) Act 2021 and UK GDPR/Data Protection Act 2018, as well as contractual requirements.

    Determining the scope of the information security management system¶

    The scope of the ISMS is defined as

    Community Fibre Limited operating within Greater London, providing broadband services to residential, business and wholesale customers; the core network infrastructure and UK customer data processing within its business software systems, supported by Customer Services, Network Technology, HR, Commercial, Finance, Marketing, Operations, Information Technology, Information Security and Data Protection.

    Exclusions to the ISMS scope

    The boundary of the ISMS Scope can be further identified by the exclusion of these specific activities:

    • The Community Fibre Limited lower tier exchanges, street cabinets, ducting, poles; training, testing and equipment and hardware storage facilities.

    • The physical security of non-Community Fibre Limited locations (e.g., customer sites, private homes) is out of scope of the ISMS. However, other security measures applied to Community Fibre Limited Information Assets that are used in these locations (e.g., laptops, mobiles) remain within scope.

    Detailed Scope description

    The ISMS scope covers Community Fibre\'s secure data handling, core network infrastructure, and aggregation sites, aligning with regulatory requirements. The Statement of Applicability details selected controls for risk treatment, justifying inclusion of all 93 Annex A controls from ISO 27001:2022. Figure 1 summarises the scope and governance structure.

    The ISMS objectives apply to all in-scope elements. There is mandatory compliance with the ISMS within this scope.

    Zones

    Community Fibre zones are numbered 1-7 in Figure 1 above.

    Z.1 Applications -- Community Fibre business applications: TCS Hobs, Apigee, Amazon Connect, Omningage, Zinier, Domo, Mobile Applications, public website, ArcGIS.

    Z.2 Data Services -- Third-party services (IaaS, PaaS) managed by Community Fibre such as AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, MongoDB and AWS RDS for the storage and processing of critical data and services such as Hobs, the public website, GIS systems and data.

    Z.3 Vendors -- Fully and partially managed SaaS applications such as Zinier, Domo, Cloudflare, BambooHR, Microsoft (M365, Azure), AWS, Google, GitHub and Gitlab, and CCI, TCS, Cognizant, Cisco, Adtran, Juniper, Palo Alto.

    Z.4 Data handling -- Data storage, data access, querying and reporting methods such as AWS RDS and API's include Google Apigee, Domo.

    Z.5 Applications and Data Interfaces -- Methods for presenting and accessing services and data including code repositories, protocols, firewalls and webservices hosted in AWS, Azure and Cloudflare.

    Z.6 Core Network and Technology -- Data centre locations, primary exchange locations, core hardware architecture, network management, automation, monitoring.

    Z.7 Identity -- Identity providers, cloud-based, on-premises, privileged access management, joiners-movers-leavers, just-in-time, role-based access management, authorisation, audit, monitoring.

    Infrastructure

    In scope:

    1. Infrastructure in the Applications zone (Zone 1) includes:

    a. Applications and containers running code that process Community Fibre Limited data.

    b. Interfaces, such as web or reporting services for accessing, querying, or downloading data.

    c. Source code

    1. Infrastructure in the Data Services zone (Zone 2), including database services, and the software and applications in each virtual compute instance.

    2. Infrastructure in the Vendors zone (Zone 3) including authentication configuration and company data stored and transferred in or out each SaaS service.

    3. Infrastructure in the Data handling zone (Zone 4) includes cloud data storage, cloud-based query logic such as APIs, and including the any endpoint devices that store company data, and any other services that store company data.

    4. Infrastructure in Data handling zone (Zone 5), used to securely deliver data or services.

    5. Infrastructure in the Core network and technology zone (Zone 6), used to deliver and manage broadband services to customer premises.

    6. Infrastructure in the Identity zone (Zone 7), used to authenticate and authorise users, vendors and customers and grant access to approved data and services when required.

    The above zones will be referred to in all ISMS documentation as the "Community Fibre infrastructure".

    Out of scope:

    1. Customer premises.

    2. Lower tier exchanges and hardware, street cabinets and hardware, ducting, poles.

    3. Training, equipment and hardware storage facilities such as Longwood. Network equipment stored at Longwood are configured at point of deployment and restored to factory default or wiped before returned to Longwood.

    People

    In scope:

    1. All staff members directly employed by Community Fibre Limited.

    2. All contractors or staff (including BPO type services) employed by service providers that have access to Community Fibre infrastructure for example CCI South Africa, TCS, Cognizant and Orange Moldova.

    3. IT and support staff and contractors working on the Community Fibre in-scope infrastructure. Contracts, service, and operating level agreements must accord with the ISMS.

    4. Suppliers and data providers who enter a contractual agreement with Community Fibre Limited.

    Out of scope:

    Users, support staff and data providers while they are not interacting with Community Fibre infrastructure or data.

    Services

    In scope:

    Services delivered on Community Fibre infrastructure can be summarised as data capture, process, access, and storage services, including:

    1. Data captured, processed, and stored through the public company website.

    2. All data acquisition activities involving external third parties.

    3. All data enrichment activities involving external third parties.

    4. All transfers of data to and from external third parties.

    5. All internal software development activities.

    Information security management system¶

    Community Fibre Limited has established, implemented, maintained, and will continually improve an information security management system in accordance with ISO27001. This ISMS provides information as to how we meet these requirements, with reference to key processes and policies, as appropriate.

    Leadership and commitment¶

    Community Fibre Limited's senior management team are committed to the development and implementation of an ISMS Policy and Information Security Management System which are both compatible with the strategic direction and the context of the organisation. In summary, the senior management team will ensure that:

    • The company has a designated senior management representative who is responsible for the maintenance and review of the Management System.

    • The ongoing activities of Community Fibre Limited are reviewed regularly and that any required corrective action is adequately implemented and reviewed to establish an effective preventative process.

    An internal audit schedule is prepared on an annual basis year and covers the requirements of the ISO27001 standard. Internal audits are carried out through "risk or clause based" auditing. Appropriate personnel are allocated to complete the internal audits and must record appropriate evidence for completeness. All audits completed must be authorised by Senior Management as complete once any non-conforming areas have been dealt with (without any undue delay). Internal audit documentation must be kept and filed appropriately.

    The ISMS management platform (ISMS.online) contains the ISO 27001 Audit Program (three years including GDPR) including ISMS internal audits 3-year plan.

    9.3 Management review¶

    Management reviews take place as a minimum on an annual basis. The attendees present are "Senior Management" and any other appropriate persons of the business.

    All inputs / outputs are fully documented, and minutes captured in line with the requirements of the specific ISO standard in which Community Fibre Limited wish to be certified. Any actions arising from the meeting must be completed without any undue delay and appropriate evidence filed with the Management review documentation.

    Improvement¶

    10.1 Continual Improvement¶

    Continual Improvement will be ongoing through various elements of the Business Management System which is encompassed within this document. The list below is not exhaustive:

    • "ISMS Risks & Treatments" - ISMS management system (ISMS.online) (See Clause 5.1, 6.1)

    • ISMS Policy and objectives

    • Customer satisfaction

    • Internal audits

    • Third-party external audits

    • Management reviews

    10.2 Nonconformity and corrective action¶

    Should any non-conformances occur or be identified then an internal audit report / non-conformance report must be completed to ensure that a full analysis of the problem is resolved. A summary of all actions will be maintained within the "ISMS Corrective Actions & Improvements" queue in Jira. The corrective action plan summary must be completed, as this then forms part of the Management Review meeting.

    Should a nonconformity occur, including those arising from complaints, internal audits & external third-party assessment Community Fibre Limited designate the appropriate "Senior Management" representative to ensure that corrective action including root cause analysis is completed and implemented to avoid any further occurrences. This is then analysed and should the risk to the business pose to be "high" then this is then entered onto the "ISMS Risks & Treatments" section of the ISMS management system (ISMS.online) (See Clause 6.1) to assist in mitigating the risk to the business.

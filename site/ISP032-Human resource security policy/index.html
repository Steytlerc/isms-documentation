    General Information¶

    This document meets the following clauses:

    • Information Security Management System BS EN ISO/IEC 27001:2022 -- A.6 People Controls

    Document Objective¶

    Community Fibre Limited has a responsibility to ensure that employees, contractors, and temporary staff are properly briefed on their information security roles and responsibilities. The objective for this document is to:

    • To ensure that employees and contractors understand their responsibilities and are suitable for the roles for which they are considered.

    • To ensure that employees and contractors are aware of and fulfil their information security responsibilities.

    • To protect the organisation's interests as part of the process of changing or terminating employment.

    Scope¶

    Community Fibre Limited employs staff, contractors, and agency staff. This policy applies to all business functions where information is used or processed, and includes functions supplied on its behalf by external contractors or suppliers. This policy is applicable to all data and information and to all people accessing Community Fibre Limited data and information from any location, regardless of the method used.

    Policy¶

    1. All Community Fibre Limited employees, whether holding a temporary, fixed term or open contract, must comply with the Information Security Policy. ISP003-Information Security Policy.pdf

    2. All employees are informed that they are required to follow Community Fibre Limited policies relating to data protection and information security when they receive their terms and conditions of employment.

    3. Violation of Community Fibre Limited's data protection or information security policies may lead to disciplinary action.

    4. Depending on the information security requirements, the Community Fibre Limited may make additional background checks or conduct additional tests during the recruitment process to assess the suitability of candidates for a role.

    5. All employees must undertake information security and data protection training during their induction to raise their awareness of the risks and issues associated with handling Community Fibre Limited information, and the appropriate safeguards. This training will be made available through Community Fibre Limited's training platform.

    6. All employees will be informed of the need to report information security and data protection incidents quickly and of the appropriate method for doing so. Periodic reminders will be issued to all employees.

    7. All employees must maintain their knowledge and skills in relation to information security throughout their employment at the Community Fibre Limited, undertaking training as required.

    8. At the request of the Head of Human Resources (or equivalent senior manager), access to information or IT systems may be removed.

    9. Employees who leave the organisation will have their access privileges terminated in line with the Access Control Policy. ISP034-Access Control Policy.pdf

    10. On leaving the Community Fibre Limited, employees must return all information assets and equipment belonging to Community Fibre Limited.

    Responsibilities¶

    1. All information users are responsible for protecting and ensuring the security of the information to which they have access.

    2. Community Fibre Limited senior management, heads of functions and line managers are responsible for ensuring that all information in their area is managed in conformance with this policy.

    3. Employees, agency staff or contractors who act in breach of this policy, or who do not act to implement it, may be subject to disciplinary procedures or other appropriate sanctions.

    Human resource security¶

    7.1 Prior to employment¶

    7.1.1 Screening¶

    Community Fibre Limited carries out background checks on all candidates for employment. This includes independent identity check (passport or similar) and confirmation of claimed academic and professional qualifications.

    7.1.2 Terms and conditions of employment¶

    As part of their terms and conditions of employment all employees, contractor and third-party users who are given access to sensitive information, should sign a confidentiality or nondisclosure agreement prior to being given access to information processing systems or facilities. (Agreements should be in place with suppliers that apply to all their staff or staff individually should accept Community Fibre Limited confidentiality and nondisclosure agreement.)

    7.2 During employment¶

    7.2.1 Management responsibilities¶

    Management shall ensure that employees:

    • Complete the mandatory online learning.

    • Are made aware of and follow all relevant information security policies.

    • Are trained appropriately to the use of any IT System.

    7.2.2 Information security awareness, education, and training¶

    All employees shall receive a formal induction process designed to introduce the organisation's security policies and expectations prior to being provided with access to information or services. Training and awareness will be delivered using a combination of methods including:

    • Companywide communications (i.e., email) campaigns

    • BambooHR Files (bamboohr.com)

    • Employee Intranet (SharePoint document library at ISMS Policies - Approved)

    • Awareness training at induction and then at set intervals

    7.2.3 Disciplinary process¶

    The Community Fibre Limited disciplinary policy is available in the Employee Handbook on BambooHR at [Files (bamboohr.com)]{.underline}.

    7.3 Termination and change of employment.¶

    Information security responsibilities and duties as described in the contract of employment remain valid after termination or change of employment shall be defined, communicated to the employee or contractor, and enforced.

    7.3.1 Termination or change of employment responsibilities.¶

    Human Resources staff or manager will notify the IT Service Desk about staff changes affecting information system access (e.g., job function changes/leaving department or organization), so that accounts may be disabled, deleted, data transferred, or access rights modified. Leavers lists will be provided to IT Service Desk to ensure all leavers' access is suspended on time.¶

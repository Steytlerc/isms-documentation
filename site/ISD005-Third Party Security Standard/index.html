    General Obligations¶

    1. Reference in this Standard to 'include(s)', 'including' or 'in particular' are deemed to have the words \"without limitation\" following them.

    2. Any reference to a directive, statute, statutory provision or subordinate legislation shall (except where the context otherwise requires) be construed as referring to such legislation as amended and in force from time- to-time and to any legislation which re-enacts or consolidates (with or without modification) any such legislation.

    3. Any reference preceded by 'M.x' is a reference to the Telecommunications Security Code of Practice.

    4. This Standard should be read in conjunction with the Telecommunications Security Code of Practice, as published on the GOV.UK website and applicable to all UK Tier 1 and Tier 2 public telecoms providers.

    b) that may arise from any other contract for the supply, provision or making available of any hardware or software, services or facilities in connection with the provision of a service, including public electronic communications network / service. (M4.01)

    1.  The Supplier must have formally documented information security
    risk assessment and management processes and procedures in
    place, which are appropriate and demonstrably followed, for the
    identification, recording, mitigation and management of security
    risks to Community Fibre Data.

2.  In relation to the Services, the Supplier will, with agreement

    Access Control¶

      1. Where a Supplier is given access to the Community Fibre Network via accounts created by Community Fibre:

    a) These must have access restricted to the Data, applications and System(s) that are required to be accessed; and

    b) the Supplier must follow Community Fibre 'joiner, mover and leaver' processes in relation to them.

    1.  Where, access to any Community Fibre System is given to Supplier
    Personnel via accounts controlled by the Supplier, in relation
    to access control the Supplier will as a minimum:

    a) ensure that a robust documented process for managing joiners, movers and leavers in a timely manner is implemented, including provision for the removal of unexpected leavers through an emergency procedure.

    b) only permit access to specific individual Supplier Personnel who have a clearly defined requirement for access, and not to groups of individuals.

    c) grant, monitor and revoke privileges only through a clear chain authority and in a timely manner.

    d) ensure that:

    i. access to any system source code is restricted to only those required to have access; and

    ii. allocation and use of privileged access rights are restricted and controlled.

    e) conduct a review of logical access control every six months to ensure that all staff have the correct access for their current role; and

    f) review privileged access on not less than a quarterly basis.

    1.  On all Supplier Systems which store, process or have the
    capability to access Community Fibre Data, in relation to access
    control, the Supplier will:

    a) immediately disable or delete the User IDs of those individuals who are no longer authorised to access any Community Fibre System;

    b) ensure that Community Fibre Data is at all times protected by System access controls to ensure that it is not accessed, modified, or deleted by unauthorised personnel;

    c) ensure that all Supplier Personnel have unique User IDs allocated to them; and

    d) establish password control Systems that ensure that passwords allocated conform to standards in accordance with Good Industry Practice and are not able to be used by unauthorised parties.

    1.  The Supplier will ensure that physical access to Community Fibre
    assets held by the Supplier is managed and protected
    appropriately, including:

    a) IT systems processing or storing Community Fibre Data to be located in an area which can only be accessed by authorised Supplier Personnel.

    b) laptops and other computers issued by Community Fibre to be kept secure by the individual to whom they are issued, and returned to Community Fibre when no longer required; and

    c) any removable media containing Community Fibre Data to be encrypted and kept locked when not in use.

    Data Security¶

      1. The Supplier will:

    a) ensure:

    i. that in relation to Community Fibre Data appropriate technical and organisational measures are taken against unauthorised or unlawful processing, accidental damage, or destruction loss.

    ii. all Community Fibre Data in transit and at rest is always protected using appropriate technical and operational controls.

    b) arrange for penetration testing - by a suitably qualified person -- of any Internet facing service that could allow access to Community Fibre Data, before deployment and after any significant change.

    c) Ensure that any assets that hold, store, or process any Community Fibre Data including physical devices, Software applications, information assets and external information Systems are:

    i. built to a defined template through cloning, use of a 'gold build', or automated deployment;

    ii. recorded on an asset register which is regularly updated and maintained; and

    iii. managed to ensure minimum information security standards are met.

    d) afford the same level of protection for printed output e.g., Customer Personal Data, reports, listings, mailings, bank, and financial details etc. as for other forms of Data i.e., restrict access, control copying and store securely.

    e) in relation to back-up and storage of Community Fibre Data:

    i. ensure that Supplier Systems and Software are backed up so that prompt recovery can take place in times of failure;

    ii. regularly make back-up copies of Community Fibre Data and Software in accordance with Good Industry Practice and as agreed with Community Fibre, and keep such backup copies secure;

    iii. document the method and location of back-ups, the type of back-ups and the volume of Community Fibre Data that is being backed-up and the reasons why that Data is being retained, in line with Good Industry Practice and any Community Fibre requirements for data retention; and

    iv. ensure that Community Fibre Data is not, and cannot be, stored on any personal devices used by Supplier Personnel.

    f) in relation to security and handling of media containing Community Fibre data, when under the Supplier's control:

    i. control and physically protect computer media (whether fixed or removable);

    ii. establish documented procedures for handling Community Fibre Data; and

    iii. control, store and dispose of computer media handled by a subcontractor in line with written procedures agreed with Community Fibre.

    g. within an agreed timeframe, securely dispose of all Community Fibre Data held by the Supplier when no longer required, using formal procedures which include:

    i. a process to identify the items that might require secure disposal;

    ii. destruction of physical media and assets in line with current industry standards and no less stringent than those set out in Appendix F;

    iii. sanitisation of any re-usable media such that Community Fibre Data is made unrecoverable;

    iv. logging disposal of assets in order to maintain an audit trail including evidence of that disposal through holding certificates of destruction for at least 12 months after destruction;

    v. returning any assets owned by Community Fibre; and

    vi. securely deleting or destroying back-up Community Fibre Data held on the written instruction of Community Fibre, save where the Supplier can demonstrate to Community Fibre's satisfaction that the Supplier is unable to do so due to a legal obligation or where required by any regulator acting within the scope of its authority.

    1.  If the Supplier has any doubts about their part in ensuring the
    continued security of Community Fibre's operations, then this
    must be raised immediately with their contact at Community
    Fibre. In particular, the Supplier must:

    a) be aware of Community Fibre's security needs which are relevant to them, and which are detailed in the contracts and in this Standard (M10.02); and

    b) understands their role where responsibilities are shared between Community Fibre and them and maintain documentation of such. (M10.03)

    1.  If Supplier intends to process, or permit the processing of,
    personal data provided by, or on behalf of Community Fibre,
    because of, or in connection with, the Supplier Contract,
    outside of the United Kingdom, Supplier may only do so:

    a) following Community Fibre's written agreement;

    b) following the undertaking of a TIA by Community Fibre, which Supplier shall contribute to, if requested; and

    c) in accordance with this Standard, the Supplier Contract and any relevant ancillary agreements e.g., a data processing agreement or data sharing agreement.

    1.  If Supplier intends to use a machine learning or artificial
    intelligence ("AI") solution for processing Community Fibre
    Data, including for training the Supplier's own models, then
    this may only be done:

    a) with specific permission from Community Fibre.

    b) if the data will be contained within a closed model / not publicly available; and

    c) with assurance that data is not used to train other models.

    Protective Technology¶

      1. If the Supplier controls the administration and operation of any specific environments and networks required for the performance of the Services and processing of any Community Fibre Data, including any Third-Party Services managed by the Supplier, it will:

    a) in relation to cryptography:

    i. only send Community Fibre Data via communications networks external to the Community Fibre network in an encrypted form;

    ii. store Community Fibre Data in an encrypted form when at rest;

    iii. ensure that, when cryptography is used, only current industry standard algorithms are used and, products which have received appropriate accreditations in accordance with Good Industry Practice;

    iv. strictly limit access to cryptographic keys to those who have a need to know, and ensure that any master keys cannot be accessed by any one individual;

    v. ensure that the information protected by encryption is transmitted over a different communication channel than the keys used to govern the cryptographic process, or use a standard and approved key exchange protocol;

    vi. ensure that cryptographic keys and related keying materials used in the process are not stored anywhere in unencrypted form; and

    vii. ensure that whenever cryptography is used, the sole readable version of Community Fibre Data is not deleted until receipt and decryption of the encrypted file has been confirmed.

    b) in relation to protection from malicious Software:

    i. implement controls to prevent and detect the introduction of malicious Software and viruses into any Systems holding Community Fibre Data or used by Community Fibre or the Supplier in relation to the Services; and

    ii. implement commercial grade anti-virus software and ensure it is kept up to date.

    c) in relation to network security controls:

    i. establish appropriate controls to ensure the security of Community Fibre Data in networks, and the protection of connected Services from unauthorised access;

    ii. implement controls to safeguard the integrity and confidentiality of Community Fibre Data passing over public networks, and to protect the connected Systems;

    iii. implement controls to protect against data leakage;

    iv. authenticate connections to remote computer Systems; and

    v. configure firewalls and gateway devices to allow only authorised traffic to flow through them.

    d) in relation to development processes:

    i. carry out Software/application development and testing with Community Fibre test Data or anonymised production Community Fibre Data where that test data has been anonymised or pseudonymised to the extent that it is considered so under the terms of the relevant Data Protection Laws to ensure it cannot be traced back to a living individual, unless otherwise agreed in writing by Community Fibre;

    ii. ensure separate transactions are used for testing or training purposes from transactions used for production processing;

    iii. where Systems are operated by the Supplier, ensure that access to compilers, assemblers, editors, and other general-purpose utilities within the test environment is restricted; and

    iv. provide assurances/warranties that none of the Software contains undocumented features or hidden mechanisms that could be used to compromise the Software\'s security.

    e) in relation to Software developed specifically for Community Fibre, the Supplier must ensure:

    i. the Software provider adheres to secure code production guidelines, which must include code inspection to verify the absence of security weaknesses;

    ii. for "off-the shelf" Software, the Supplier must satisfy itself that any Software licence adequately describes the Software capabilities, which must be validated by the Supplier's own security testing at the Supplier's own cost;

    iii. to obtain written confirmation from any third-party providing Software in relation to the Services that the Software will not require the modification or renewal of existing Community Fibre computer security Systems; and

    iv. to not utilise any free or downloaded code not controlled by the Supplier (e.g., shared code-banks, repositories from on-line sources that may harbour unknown procedures, malware, or access rights).

    Security Monitoring¶

      1. If requested by Community Fibre in relation to the Services provided, the Supplier will provide assurance that newly identified vulnerabilities are mitigated within defined timescales no longer than those outlined in Appendix E. Compliance must be demonstrated using supporting evidence at Community Fibre's request.

    a) stored centrally, away from source and protected from unauthorised access, where central storage of logs is not possible, logs must be securely stored locally;

    b) maintained with sufficient quality to be used as admissible evidence during forensic investigation;

    c) reviewed regularly to ensure the admissibility of evidence as part of any forensic investigations; and

    1.  protected against tampering and unauthorised access. The
    supplier must ensure that appropriate technical controls are in
    place to prevent the accidental or malicious exfiltration of
    Community Fibre Data via external electronic communication
    channels such as USB mass storage devices, email, and web-based
    file transfer sites e.g., Data Loss Prevention, Web filtering
    and email filtering.

    Incident Management¶

    1. The Supplier will ensure that Supplier Personnel are aware of their roles in identifying, reporting, and responding to information security events or incidents.

    2. If the Supplier becomes aware of any actual, potential, or attempted Breach of Security; or any breach of its obligations under this Third-Party Security Standard, it will immediately:

    a) notify Community Fibre incident management team and the relevant business partner at Community Fibre of the actual or suspected breaches of security of its obligations under this Third-Party Security Standard and report on the potential effect of such breach to Community Fibre as soon as possible (and, in any event, within 24 hours);

    b) identify a single point of contact for Community Fibre to liaise with during the management of the security incident;

    c) take immediate steps to contain any breach to prevent further impact as appropriate;

    d) take all reasonable steps necessary, as approved by Community Fibre, to remedy such breach (to the extent that it is capable of remediation) or protect Community Fibre Data against any such potential or attempted breach or threat, promptly investigate such breach and provide Community Fibre with regular updates throughout;

    e) advise Community Fibre of any Supplier Personnel implicated in the incident and take appropriate disciplinary action, including suspension if required, upon Community Fibre's request;

    f) promptly take measures to ensure that such breach does not occur again and provide a written report to Community Fibre detailing the measures taken;

    g) document the response to the matter and finding of the investigation into such breach and issue this to Community Fibre as soon as practical whilst continuing to provide investigation and remediation action point updates to Community Fibre, until such time they are completed to the satisfaction of Community Fibre;

    h) where criminal activity is suspected, or a breach of contractual or regulatory requirements and, if requested by Community Fibre:

    i. engage an independent third-party approved by Community Fibre to gather legally admissible evidence or contact the law enforcement agencies;

    ii. allow Community Fibre access to conduct its own investigations or work with the engaged third-party; and

    iii. ensure all communications with third parties or the media are agreed in writing by Community Fibre before release.

     1.  Any costs that Community Fibre incurs that are associated with
     any Breach of Security response or investigation will be
     charged back to the Supplier, including audit costs and
     expenses.

2.  If applicable, the Supplier will ensure that relevant response

    Appendix A -- Applicable for Personal or Strictly Confidential Data¶

    The following requirements apply if the Supplier has been authorised by Community Fibre to store, process or transmit any Customer Personal Data including sensitive personal data or Special Category Data or any other data classified as Strictly Confidential by Community Fibre. This could include but is not limited to the following types of data:

    • Personal Details - name, date of birth, salary, nationality

    • Account Details - Call line identification, devices ID, IP address, cookies, service history

    • Company Data -- company contact details, county court judgements, bankruptcy information

    • Financial Details -- Bank details, payment account history, historic bills

    • Interaction History -- copies of correspondence, call recordings, chat transcripts, engineer fault notices

    d) organisational roles delegated responsibility for these controls and security measures.

    annually and provided to Community Fibre on request.

2.  The supplier will provide information security awareness and

3.  The Supplier must have established priorities for business and
    security objectives.

4.  The Supplier will:

    a) ensure that appropriate information security training and awareness is conducted for all Supplier Personnel upon hire and at least annually, which includes content around the handling of Customer Personal Data in line with the appropriate legislation; and

    b) nominate a member of its personnel as its security officer to act as a single point of contact for all security related matters between the Supplier and Community Fibre.

    Risk Management¶

      1. A security risk assessment of Contact Centre and BPO operations and facilities shall be conducted and repeated on an annual basis or at the point of change of the service provided and the supplier shall ensure that any risks to Community Fibre Data which emerge are treated and monitored on an ongoing basis in accordance with best practice in risk management. The findings will be retained for at least twelve months after closure and be made available to Community Fibre upon request.

      1. Appropriate training will be given to all staff with access to Community Fibre Data to ensure that:

    a) all legal and statutory obligations are understood and complied with along with Community Fibre specified procedures for ensuring compliance including, but not limited to, the Data Protection Laws and the Computer Misuse Act 1990;

    b) Community Fibre Data is processed in a secure manner within the contact centre and BPO environment in compliance with this schedule, the general requirements set out in the Third-Party Security Standard other relevant Community Fibre and Contact Centre and BPO policies, procedures, and practices;

    c) Contact Centre Operatives know how to identify potentially fraudulent calls, suspicious behaviours and attempted social engineering techniques and how to respond and report such attempts; and

    e) The training delivered will have a test element with a pass mark to ensure that all staff have assimilated the messages delivered sufficiently to process Community Fibre data competently and securely. Where the testing element is failed front line access will be revoked until completed successfully.

    f) Such training will be delivered on employment, before access to the Contact Centre and BPO Environment and Community Fibre Data, and annually thereafter.

    g) A record of each employee's record of training will be retained for the period of employment of the employee and 12 months thereafter.

      1. All staff with either direct or indirect access to Community Fibre Data should be subject to preemployment background checks and vetting procedures in accordance with best practice to ensure they do not present a risk of fraudulent activity or unauthorised disclosure, amendment, or destruction of such data.

      2. Such checks shall be re-performed, at a frequency to be agreed with Community Fibre, considering the level of risk identified because of initial checks.

      3. A formal disciplinary process shall be established to address breaches of security policy within the call centre and BPO location.

    Acceptable Use¶

      1. Access to non-Community Fibre approved external email, social media, online storage, and other modes of external electronic communication shall be restricted while processing Community Fibre Data to reduce the potential for external distribution of such data.

      2. Where Community Fibre data is stored and processed outside of Community Fibre's own systems and environment access to webmail, social media, file-sharing sites, gambling sites and pornographic sites will be prohibited, unless authorised by Community Fibre and will be blocked using web-filtering controls.

    Identification Verification¶

      1. The correct level of identification verification as determined by Community Fibre's Access Control Policy will be performed before disclosing customer data on a call, processing a customer transaction, or amending customer account details.

      2. Where possible advanced identification and verification techniques such as passive voice biometrics, DTMF (Dual Tone Multi Frequency) verification or phone printing shall be used to assure the identity of the caller while reducing security risk.

    Use of Mobile Devices¶

      1. All mobile devices, except were authorised by Community Fibre, including those capable of capturing Community Fibre Data via photography, audio, or other recording mechanism such as tablets, phones, smart watches, cameras, and Dictaphones will not be taken by contact centre and BPO staff into any environment where Community Fibre customer calls or Community Fibre Data is being processed. They will be stored securely prior to entering the call centre environment to prevent access until staff leave this environment.

      2. Where so authorised, appropriate processes will be in place to ensure that those mobile devices are used in an appropriate manner.

    Transfer and Recording of Community Fibre Data¶

      1. Community Fibre Data will only be processed for the purpose or purposes of providing the services specified.

      2. Community Fibre Data will only be stored in authorised systems, applications and databases and must not be downloaded or transferred from such systems, saved locally, or moved onto supplier systems without approval from Community Fibre.

      3. Community Fibre Data will not be written down, photographed, printed, or photocopied.

      4. Only Community Fibre approved note taking mechanisms such as the Knowledge notepad function will be employed within the Contact Centre Environment with such mechanisms being wiped after a period agreed by Community Fibre.

      5. Community Fibre Data will not be transferred onto removable media under any circumstances. Removable media drives within endpoints will be disabled or controlled to prevent such transfer.

      6. The use of any email service is prohibited unless this has been approved by Community Fibre. Where email services are approved, technical restrictions will prevent these email services being accessible outside of Community Fibre approved locations. The domains of incoming and outgoing emails will be restricted to only those approved by Community Fibre.

      7. Internet access will be restricted to only Community Fibre approved URLs and Domains.

      8. If two headsets or phones are being used (i.e., for training or monitoring purposes) both lines will be recorded in case the secondary staff member takes over the call.

      9. Calls to physical or softphones cannot be transferred or diverted to a mobile or external number.

    Physical Security¶

      1. Appropriate physical security controls shall be in place to prevent unauthorised access to the Contact Centre and BPO Environment and Community Fibre data held within it. This will include but is not limited to:

    a) Physical perimeter security controls such as gates, fences, and manned entry points;

    b) A documented and enforced clear desk policy;

    c) A requirement for all staff to visibly wear ID badges at all times within the Contact Centre

    d) Control of entry points by access control mechanisms such as turnstiles to prevent tailgating;

    e) physical security zoning with appropriate access control between sensitive areas such as the contact centre floor, data centres, comms rooms and back-office areas to ensure that there is no inappropriate access to areas where customer data is processed;

    f) Locking screens when away from desk or automatically when there is a period of inactivity e.g., 10 minutes;

    g) Computer screens which will be used to access customer data must not be visible from outside the building. Where this is not possible privacy screens will be used to restrict the view of such screens;

    h) CCTV will be in operation and monitored within the Contact Centre. The footage must be stored for a minimum of 90-days or as per the legal jurisdiction requirements;

    i) Printing will be disabled;

    j) Any Laptops used will be secured to the desks at the working location; and

    k) Laptops are prohibited to be taken off-site unless approved by Community Fibre in writing.

    1.  A physical perimeter (outer) and security (inner) assessment
    shall be conducted on an annual basis as part of the risk
    assessment required at 3.1 of this appendix.

    Access Management¶

      1. Staff with access to Community Fibre Data must undergo (and then repeat regularly) security awareness training along with all other necessary training to ensure that they can use systems storing Community Fibre Data competently prior to being given access to ensure the security and integrity of Community Fibre Data.

      2. Appropriate processes and technical controls shall be in place to ensure that physical and logical access to the contact centre environment and Community Fibre Data is restricted to authorised staff on the basis of the principles of "least privilege", appropriate segregation of duties and "need to know".

      3. Role based, and where possible workflow based, access will be implemented to limit access to excessive amounts of data.

      4. Multi-factor authentication will be implemented for access to systems and application which will allow access to Community Fibre Customer Personal Data.

      5. All contact centre and BPO staff accessing Community Fibre Data will do so using a unique account linked to their identity which has been verified through appropriate background checks.

      6. Contact centre and BPO staff will not access Community Fibre Data through generic or shared accounts and will not share unique accounts, passwords or other means of authentication which allow access with other staff members, contractors, contingent workers, or any other person.

      7. Where a member of staff leaves the organisation in an unplanned manner, for example due to gross misconduct, their access shall be disabled immediately. Any known passwords shall be reset.

      8. Where a member of staff is absent from work for 3 working days without explanation their access must be disabled.

      9. Where a member of staff is absent from work for longer than 30 days for any reason their access should be disabled.

The Supplier will accept and apply security controls as directed by Community Fibre, which are at least as rigorous as those applied by Community Fibre within its own Systems, equipment, services and facilities. (M10.17) Additionally, employees and contractors working for the Supplier who access the network, equipment, services, or facilities shall notify Community Fibre of any security concerns or flaws in defined access methods.

      3. The Supplier acknowledges Community Fibre must retain control and oversight of the Community Fibre Network and Community Fibre Data. (M10.05) Where any transfers of control, or of Community Fibre Data occur the following restrictions shall apply: (M10.08)

    a) The Supplier shall only request, or permit, control of elements of the Community Fibre Network when absolutely necessary. Any transfer of control shall be limited to the necessary and defined purpose.

    b) Only remote access methods that have been defined and agreed with Community Fibre, and are owned and operated by Community Fibre, shall be used. (M10.22)

    c) The Supplier shall only request, or permit, transfer of Community Fibre Data to their Systems when absolutely necessary. Where a transfer of Data is necessary it shall be through a defined process.

    d) The Supplier acknowledges that where network or Community Fibre Data leaves Community Fibre's control, Community Fibre shall contractually require that the Data is protected and will periodically verify the protections that are in place.) This shall include the Supplier ensuring that Data is only visible or accessible to appropriate employees and from appropriate locations. (M10.09)

    e) The Supplier shall only use an encrypted and authenticated channel when transferring Community Fibre Data. (M10.10)

6.  Community Fibre requires the Supplier to:

    a) implement:

    i. technical controls which will prevent any of the Supplier's clients or their network from adversely affecting any other client or their network; (M10.24)

    ii. logical separation within their network to segregate the data and networks of each of the Supplier's different clients; (M10.25)

    iii. separation between management environments used for the Supplier's different clients; (M10.26)

    iv. security enforcing functions at the boundary between the Supplier's network and Community Fibre's network; (M10.27)

    v. technical controls to limit the potential for users or systems to negatively impact more than one of the Supplier's clients; (M10.28)

    vi. logically independent privileged access workstations that are not used for any other client; (M10.29)

    vii. independent administrative domains and accounts for each of the Suppliers clients; (M10.30)

    b) monitor and audit the activities of the Supplier's staff when accessing or administering the Community Fibre Network; (M10.33)

    c) obtain, store, retain and make available to Community Fibre on demand, all logs relating to the security of the Supplier's network and Supplier Systems to the extent that such logs relate to access into Community Fibre's network for at least 13 months prior to the request date; (M10.34)

    d) ensure that if the Supplier's networks or any of the Supplier Systems, including user endpoints, could impact Community Fibre then they have the same level of testing that Community Fibre applies to its own networks; (M10.35) and without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing:

    i. the Supplier shall conduct an annual red teaming event equivalent to TBEST, along with prior testing of major releases / new systems / significant changes to Systems (networks, systems, applications, etc.) based on assessment of security requirements so that all implemented network security functions are tested sufficiently to simulate a competent attacker; and

    ii. the testing shall be conducted by an independent external party that has CREST and CHECK certification -- or OSCP / OSCE, CRT / CTL, CRTO or at least five years of experience where the testing is done by an individual.

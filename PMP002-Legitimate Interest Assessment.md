### Introduction {#introduction .Heading-3_Green}

> This Legitimate Interest Policy outlines the framework under which
> Community Fibre Limited processes personal data based on the legal
> basis of \"Legitimate Interests\" as per the Data Protection Act 2018
> and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

### Scope {#scope .Heading-3_Green}

> This policy applies to all personal data processed by Community Fibre
> Limited and its subsidiaries, where the legal basis for such
> processing is legitimate interest.

### Definitions {#definitions .Heading-3_Green}

- **Personal Data**: Any information relating to an identified or
  identifiable natural person.

- **Processing**: Any operation performed on personal data, whether
  automated or not.

- **Legitimate Interests**: A legal basis for processing personal data,
  other than consent, contract, or legal obligation

### Identification of Legitimate Interests {#identification-of-legitimate-interests .Heading-3_Green}

Before processing any personal data based on legitimate interests,
\[Your Company Name\] will:

- Clearly identify the legitimate interests pursued.

- Conduct a Legitimate Interests Assessment (LIA) to ensure that these
  interests are not overridden by the data subject's interests, rights,
  or freedoms.

### Legitimate Interests Assessment (LIA) {#legitimate-interests-assessment-lia .Heading-3_Green}

The LIA will include:

- **Purpose Test**: Identify the legitimate interest behind the
  processing.

- **Necessity Tes**t: Ensure that the processing is necessary to achieve
  the identified interests.

- **Balancing Tes**t: Ensure that the legitimate interests are not
  overridden by the data subject\'s interests, rights, or freedoms.

### Data Minimization {#data-minimization .Heading-3_Green}

Community Fibre Limited will only process data that is strictly
necessary for the identified legitimate interests.

### Transparency {#transparency .Heading-3_Green}

Data subjects will be informed about the processing activities based on
legitimate interests through privacy notices or other appropriate means.

### Data Subject Rights {#data-subject-rights .Heading-3_Green}

Data subjects have the right to object to processing based on legitimate
interests. Upon receiving an objection, \[Your Company Name\] will
immediately cease processing unless it can demonstrate compelling
legitimate grounds for the processing.

### Record-keeping {#record-keeping .Heading-3_Green}

All LIAs and related documentation will be kept on record and made
available for inspection by regulatory authorities.

### Review {#review .Heading-3_Green}

#### This policy will be reviewed at least annually or whenever there is a significant change in processing activities.
